## 2026-10-24 AD9226 IQ root-cause follow-up

The connected converter was confirmed by the user to be AD9226.  Two previous
EBAZ4205 projects were inspected: `ebaz4205-ad9235-eth-sdr` is an older 40 MHz
prototype (its nested `common` checkout is commit `37002d0`) and contains a
13-to-12-bit ADC concatenation error; `ebaz4205-ad9226` uses the later
`common` commit `2c05b8c`, a 65 MHz clock with a 180-degree forwarded output,
and feeds the raw 12-bit bus to signed DSP.  The latter is the reliable coding
reference, while clock polarity was established separately on the K9+ board.

Live A/B captures isolated three independent faults:

- Flipping ADC bit 11 produced a half-scale discontinuity: I/Q correlation was
  approximately 0.99999994 and scan RMS was 27..35 dB above raw-bus captures.
  The fitted module is two's-complement, so the MSB inversion was removed.
- The original IQ right shift of 9 discarded about 24 dB of usable transport
  range.  Shift 5 gives RMS 1692.6 and peak 5750 at 12 MHz / gain `0x40`, with
  zero I16 clipping and ADC OTR false.
- Inverting the forwarded 40 MHz clock stopped IQ packet production on this
  exact mezzanine.  Restoring the empirically verified in-phase clock restored
  continuous streaming.  AD9226 3.5..7.0 ns clock-to-data limits are now in
  the XDC; final timing is WNS +2.029 ns, WHS +0.047 ns, TNS/THS 0.

The corrected 12 MHz capture has I/Q correlation 0.0185; a 2.46-second capture
at 7.8 MHz / gain `0x60` has correlation -0.00067 and image metric about
-62.8 dB.  A 1..20 MHz scan completed with 512/512 packets at every center,
no sequence gaps and no FPGA drops.  The strongest short-wave candidate in
the present RF environment is 8.00437 MHz (about 24 dB above median in the
7.8 MHz window); most other observed lines are a roughly 69 kHz interference
comb rather than broadcast-channel-spaced carriers.  The digital ADC/DFE/IQ
transport is therefore operational; remaining reception quality is dominated
by the analog input/antenna environment and these local spurs.

The installed HDSDR DLL identifies `K9+ AD9226`; its High preset is `0x60`
(`0x80` has gain code zero and mutes the AD8370 high range).  Final live smoke:
4,904 callbacks, 2,510,848 complex samples, RMS 484.897, peak 3041.  The
temporary test-only firewall rule was removed afterward.

### tinySA injected-signal proof

A tinySA source was connected to the ADC board at 9.9 MHz, -76 dBm, AM 1 kHz.
With the DFE centered at 9.8 MHz and AD8370 code `0x60`, a 2.4576-second IQ
capture measured:

- carrier 9,899,959.37 Hz (about -40.63 Hz from the generator setting),
  50.54 dB above the spectral median;
- lower/upper sidebands 996.59 Hz and 991.82 Hz from the carrier, at
  -15.73 dBc and -15.80 dBc respectively;
- I/Q correlation -0.000196, zero I16 clipping, and zero UDP/FPGA drops.

After reprogramming to clear the sticky status, the same source and `0x60`
gain produced `adc_overrange=false`.  This proves that the complete AD9226,
DFE, IQ and Ethernet path preserves the injected AM modulation.  Failure to
hear this tone in HDSDR is downstream in ExtIO selection, HDSDR demodulation
or Windows audio configuration, not in FPGA RF acquisition.

### 15.230 MHz live-station/RSP1 comparison

The user heard clear broadcast audio at 15.230 MHz with an RSP1 but initially
reported only a narrow peak from the FPGA receiver. Three direct FPGA captures
centered at 15.130, 15.230 and 15.330 MHz placed the same carrier at about
+99.96, 0 and -100.04 kHz. Its inferred RF frequency was 15.22996 MHz. This
proves the FPGA tune FCW, mixer sign and frequency metadata are correct.

The carrier rose normally with AD8370 gain and reached about 20--22 dB above
the spectral-bin median around codes `0xa0`--`0xc0`, but its audio sidebands
were buried in broadband noise. A 3.36-second capture had no UDP/FPGA drops or
I16 clipping (I/Q peaks approximately 13k), yet AM envelope analysis did not
recover intelligible speech. `0xc0` asserted sticky OTR in one run and `0xe0`
asserted it; after reprogramming, the final `0xa0` / 15.230 MHz / 512-packet
check was clean with `adc_overrange=false`. The remaining issue is therefore
analog sensitivity, blocking, antenna/feedline equivalence or local EMI, not a
known DFE or Ethernet defect. See the README handoff section for the ordered
next experiments and the retained capture filenames.
